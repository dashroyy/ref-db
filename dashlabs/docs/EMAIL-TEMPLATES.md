# Email templates

Copy, adjust, send. Replace the parts in [brackets]. They match what the website promises (quote first, preview before
printing, invoice to pay), so customers hear the same thing from you as they read on the site.

## 1. Reply to an order request (cart items)

Subject: Your DashLabs order [DL-000000-XXXX]

Hi [name],

Thanks for your order! Here's the total:

- [items, as listed in their email]
- Shipping to [place]: $[x]
- **Total: $[x]**

I'll send a secure invoice in a moment; you can pay by card. Printing starts as soon as it's paid, and your order
should ship by [date].

Thanks,
[your name], DashLabs

## 2. Quote for a custom piece

Subject: Your DashLabs quote: [what it is]

Hi [name],

Thanks for the photos, this will be a fun one. Here's the quote:

- [what you'll make, size, finish]: $[x]
- Shipping: $[x]
- **Total: $[x]**

How it works from here:

1. If you're happy, I'll send an invoice for [the total / a 50% deposit].
2. I sculpt the model and send you a 3D preview within [x] days.
3. You approve it or ask for changes (one round is included).
4. I print, finish and ship it, about [x] days after approval.

Any questions, just reply.

[your name], DashLabs

## 3. Preview ready

Subject: Your preview is ready

Hi [name],

Here's the preview of your [piece] (attached). Have a good look from every angle.

- Happy? Reply "approved" and it goes to the printer.
- Want changes? Tell me what, as specifically as you can. One round is included.

[your name], DashLabs

## 4. Shipped

Subject: Your DashLabs order is on its way

Hi [name],

Your [item] is printed, checked and on its way. Tracking: [link or number].

A few care tips: keep it out of hot cars and direct sun, and clean it with lukewarm water and a soft brush.

If anything arrives damaged, send me a photo within 14 days and I'll put it right.

[your name], DashLabs

## 5. Review request (about a week after delivery)

Subject: How did it turn out?

Hi [name],

I hope the [item] arrived safely. If you have a minute, I'd love to hear what you think, and to see a photo of it
in its new home. With your OK I'd share your words (and photo) on the site.

Thanks again for ordering from a one-person lab.

[your name], DashLabs

## 6. Can't print this (licence)

Subject: About your print request

Hi [name],

Thanks for asking. I checked the design you linked, and its licence only allows personal use, so I'm not able to sell
prints of it. Two options: ask the designer if they offer a commercial licence, or I can suggest a similar design I am
licensed to print.

[your name], DashLabs
